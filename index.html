Privacy Policy — Chariot

Last updated: 06/11/2026

Chariot ("we", "our", or "us") is a Bhagavad Gita learning app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Locally on your device: Your reading progress, quiz scores, streak count, bookmarks, and app preferences are stored locally using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to sign in.

If you sign in (optional): We store your email address and display name in Firebase to sync your progress across devices. We do not sell this data.

Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use Chariot and improve the learning experience. This includes events like verses read, quizzes completed, levels finished, and features used. Analytics data is aggregated and does not include personally identifiable information. We also use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability.

Feedback: If you submit feedback through the app, your message and user ID (if signed in) are stored in Firebase to help us improve Chariot. You can also submit feedback anonymously.

Notifications: If you grant permission, we send daily reminders at your chosen time. You can disable these in Settings at any time.

Advertising ID (Android only): On Android devices, we collect the Advertising ID to measure app install attribution and understand how users discover Chariot. This ID can be reset or disabled in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We do not use it to serve personalized ads.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect payment information (the app is free)
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers
  • We do not sell any data to third parties

Third-Party Services

Chariot uses the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, analytics, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign In — If you sign in with Apple. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign In — If you sign in with Google. Google Privacy Policy

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted immediately. Anonymous crash logs are automatically deleted by Firebase after 90 days.

Children

Chariot is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (visible in the app's Profile section)
  • Delete your data (see our Data Deletion page)
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling app tracking in your device settings
  • Disable notifications at any time in Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last updated" date above.
