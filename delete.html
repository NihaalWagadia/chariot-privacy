Delete Your Data — Chariot

App: Chariot: Bhagavad Gita Daily

Developer: Chariot Apps

Last updated: 06/11/2026

You can request deletion of your data at any time using either of the methods below.

Method 1 — Delete From Within the App (Instant)

This is the fastest way. It immediately deletes all your data from the app and our servers:

Step 1 — Open the Chariot app
Step 2 — Tap Profile on the bottom navigation bar
Step 3 — Tap Settings (gear icon)
Step 4 — Scroll to Account Actions
Step 5 — Tap Delete Account and confirm twice

Your data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Method 2 — Request via Email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at: chariot.gita.app@gmail.com

Include: your display name and the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

Data Type What Happens Retention Period
Email address Permanently deleted from Firebase Deleted immediately
Display name Permanently deleted Deleted immediately
Reading progress, XP, streaks Permanently deleted from device and servers Deleted immediately
Quiz scores and bookmarks Permanently deleted Deleted immediately
Feedback submissions Permanently deleted from Firebase Deleted immediately
Analytics events Anonymous and aggregated — not linked to your account. Cannot be individually deleted. Auto-deleted by Firebase after 14 months
Crash logs (Crashlytics) Anonymous — not linked to your account. Cannot be individually deleted. Auto-deleted by Firebase after 90 days

Local Data

Data stored only on your device (if you never signed in) can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in your device settings.
